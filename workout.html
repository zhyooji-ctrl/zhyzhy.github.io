Pull‑up Progression (to get your first rep):
  • 1. Negative pull‑ups: Jump to the top, lower yourself as slowly as possible (5–8 sec). 3 sets of 5 reps.
  • 2. Band‑assisted: Use a resistance band under knees/feet. 3 sets of 8–12 reps.
  • 3. Inverted rows: Feet on floor, pull chest to bar. 3 sets of 10–15 reps.
  • 4. Jumping pull‑ups: Use momentum to get chin over bar. 3 sets of 8 reps.
  • 5. Full pull‑ups: Aim for 3 sets of max reps. Once you can do 3–5, add weight.
📆 Do this 3x per week (on your gym days) after your main lifts.
🔥 Add 5 lb to upper lifts / 10 lb to squats weekly. If you stall → eat more carbs pre-workout.
